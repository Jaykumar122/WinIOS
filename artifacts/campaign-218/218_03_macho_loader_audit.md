# WINIOS CAMPAIGN 218 — STAGE 218.3 MACH-O LOADER CAPABILITY AUDIT

**Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Campaign:** 218 (Stage 218.3)  
**Host:** Windows 11 x64, Visual Studio 2026, MSVC C++20 (`/W4 /WX /permissive-`)  
**Cross Compiler:** Windows Zig 0.17.0 (`-target aarch64-macos`)  
**Target Profile:** iPhone 15 (`iPhone15,4` / `D37AP` / Apple A16 Bionic `T8120`), 16 KB Granule  
**Component Audited:** `src/loader/macho_user_loader.h` & `src/loader/macho_user_loader.cpp`

---

## 1. Executive Summary

An audit of `MachoUserLoader` was performed against the genuine 64-bit ARM64 Mach-O binaries generated by the Windows Zig 0.17.0 toolchain (`-target aarch64-macos`). 

Inspection of compiler-generated Mach-O binaries revealed 17 load commands totaling 1,064 bytes:
1. `LC_SEGMENT_64 (__PAGEZERO)` (vmaddr 0x0, vmsize 4GB, no perms)
2. `LC_SEGMENT_64 (__TEXT)` (vmaddr 0x100000000, 16KB aligned, initprot=5 RX)
3. `LC_SEGMENT_64 (__DATA_CONST)` (vmaddr 0x100004000, 16KB aligned, initprot=3 RW -> RO)
4. `LC_SEGMENT_64 (__DATA)` (vmaddr 0x100008000, 16KB aligned, initprot=3 RW)
5. `LC_SEGMENT_64 (__LINKEDIT)` (vmaddr 0x10000c000, 16KB aligned, initprot=1 R)
6. `LC_DYLD_INFO_ONLY`
7. `LC_SYMTAB`
8. `LC_DYSYMTAB`
9. `LC_LOAD_DYLINKER`
10. `LC_MAIN` (entryoff=0x1448, stacksize=0x1000000)
11. `LC_SOURCE_VERSION`
12. `LC_BUILD_VERSION`
13. `LC_DATA_IN_CODE`
14. `LC_CODE_SIGNATURE`
15. `LC_LOAD_DYLIB`
16. `UNKNOWN (0x26, LC_FUNCTION_STARTS)`
17. `UNKNOWN (0x1b)`
